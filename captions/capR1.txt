Dumpasur tab paida hota hai jab paper, plastic, metal aur e-waste sab ek hi bore mein bhar dete ho. Mila hua kabaad na theek se tulta hai, na recycle hota hai.

Iska ilaaj simple hai: 3 thaile. Sukha paper ek mein, dhula plastic doosre mein, metal aur e-waste teesre mein. Battery hamesha alag.

Aur haan, kachra jalana mana hai. Dhuaan Kanpur ki hawa ka nuksaan hai.

Happy Diwali, Kanpur.
