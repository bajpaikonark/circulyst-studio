Raddi bechne se pehle ye 4 sawaal zaroor poochho. Diwali ki safai mein kaam aayenge.

1. Rate pehle bataya? Tolne ke baad rate sunoge toh mol-bhaav ka khel shuru.
2. Kaanta digital hai? Vajan aapki aankhon ke saamne screen pe dikhna chahiye.
3. Receipt milegi? Kya diya, kitna tula, kis rate pe, sab likha ho.
4. Paisa kab aur kaise? Circulyst mein 70% wahin ke wahin milega, baaki verification ke baad, aur dono receipt pe likha hoga.

Circulyst jald Kanpur mein scrap pickup ke saath ye chaaron pakke lekar aa raha hai. Comment KANPUR karo, early access list ka link DM mein aayega.
