Diwali ki safai mein ye 5 niyam yaad rakhna:
1. Raddi sukhi rakho. Geeli raddi kisi kaam ki nahi.
2. Gatta flat karo aur bundle bana ke baandh do.
3. Plastic dho ke sukhao, phir alag thaile mein.
4. Battery, phone, charger alag. Kachre mein kabhi nahi.
5. Kuch bhi jalao mat. Na kachra, na patte.

Circulyst jald Kanpur mein aa raha hai.
