Diwali ki safai mein sab kabaad ek bore mein mat bharo. 3 thaile banao.

Thaila 1, paper: akhbaar, copy kitaab, gatte ke dibbe. Sukha rakho, geela paper kam bikta hai.

Thaila 2, plastic: bottles, dabbe, polythene. Dho ke sukhao.

Thaila 3, metal aur e-waste: tin, bartan, taar, purana phone aur charger. Battery hamesha alag rakho.

Alag kiya hua kabaad jaldi tulta hai aur hisaab saaf rehta hai. Circulyst Kanpur mein digital kaanta aur har pickup ki receipt lekar aa raha hai.
